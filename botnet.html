Here's a breakdown of its meaning and history:

Mathematical Origin: The infinity symbol was first introduced in a mathematical context by English mathematician John Wallis in 1655. In mathematics, it's used to describe a quantity that is larger than any natural number or a process that continues indefinitely. For example, it is used in limits to describe a variable that grows arbitrarily large.

Symbolic Meaning: Beyond mathematics, the infinity symbol has taken on powerful metaphorical meanings. It is widely used to represent concepts like eternity, everlasting love, and empowerment. Its shape, a continuous loop with no beginning or end, makes it a fitting representation of these ideas.

Ancient Roots: While Wallis introduced the mathematical symbol, the concept of infinity and similar shapes have appeared throughout history. An ancient symbol called the Ouroboros, which depicts a serpent or dragon eating its own tail, is also representative of infinity, immortality, and eternal return.

Modern Usage: Because of its elegant shape and profound meaning, the infinity symbol is a popular design in jewelry and tattoos to symbolize an unending bond or eternal love. It has also been used in modern mysticism and literary symbology
