THE GAME / UPLINK, 2001

Uplink is a hacking simulation by Introversion Software. You are a freelance agent renting gateway time from a corporation and taking contracts against other corporations: copying files, planting evidence, laundering money, and erasing your own trail before a trace completes. It is still sold and still patched today, and its mission statement is simple — you play either to win or to learn.

THIS DEMO / FAITH — REVELATION

Three in-game hours built around one question: do you like Faith, or do you need Revelation to do so? Hour one is fully interactive — every day you choose the job, the tool and the password, and every job is harmless: sweetener instead of sugar, an overpaid grocery bill, loyalty points that were expiring anyway.

At 01:00 the question returns and only BOTH answers. Hour two is a cutscene where other agents log in for the first time and make their own choices. Hour three is strongly guided and cannot be failed: Faith stays on your machine, Revelation is copied to a floppy and never run, and both dormant files are handed to the internal service machines so their users decide for themselves. After 03:00 the demo enters overtime, where every day you keep playing is a new end scene — until the day you are caught.

Fan-made and unofficial. Uplink is a trademark of Introversion Software and is sold on Valve's Steam platform. This demo contains no original game assets, makes no real network connections and is provided free of charge by inovation.co.nl. If you enjoy the idea, buy the real thing.
