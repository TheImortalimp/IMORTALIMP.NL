🥔 Simple Potato Soup Recipes

Welcome to your cozy, nourishing soup collection! All soups here use potatoes as the base and stick to basic ingredients — just salt, pepper, a touch of vinegar, and no special herbs. Oil and dairy are included sparingly for balance. No eggs needed 🍲

🥕 Classic Cabbage & Potato Soup

  • 3 potatoes, peeled & diced
  • 2 cups chopped cabbage
  • 1 carrot (or frozen), chopped
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 1 tsp vinegar (optional)
  • Boil all until tender (~25 mins)

🧄 Garlic & Pea Potato Soup

  • 3 potatoes, diced
  • 1 cup sweet peas (frozen or canned)
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt, pepper, and a splash of vinegar
  • Sauté garlic, then boil all until soft

🧅 Creamy Onion Potato Soup

  • 3 potatoes, diced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 cup milk (any kind)
  • 1 tsp oil or butter
  • Salt & pepper
  • Cook onion in oil, add potato, water, simmer, then stir in milk at end

🌽 Corn & Potato Soup

  • 3 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 cup corn (frozen or canned)
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt, pepper, vinegar to taste
  • Boil all until soft and sweet

🥬 Potato & Sauerkraut Soup

  • 3 potatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup sauerkraut, rinsed lightly
  • 1 tsp oil or butter
  • Salt & pepper
  • Simmer together for a sour-savory dish

🥣 Potato Milk Broth Soup

  • 3 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 cup milk
  • Salt, pepper
  • Boil potatoes in water, mash a few pieces, add milk at end and warm through

🍋 Lemon Potato Soup

  • 3 potatoes, cubed
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • Salt & pepper
  • Boil all together and stir in lemon juice before serving

🍜 Soy Sauce Veggie Potato Soup

  • 3 potatoes, diced
  • 1 cup mixed frozen vegetables (carrot, green beans, corn)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • Salt & pepper (taste first!)
  • Simmer all together and serve hot

🥦 Broccoli & Potato Soup

  • 3 potatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup broccoli florets (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt & pepper
  • Boil until everything is tender

🍠 Sweet Potato & White Potato Mix

  • 2 white potatoes, 1 sweet potato
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt, pepper, and lemon juice to finish
  • Boil until smooth and colorful

💡 Tip: Add vinegar, lemon, or soy sauce near the end of cooking for brightness and depth. Soups can be kept in the fridge for 2–3 days. 🧊

Stay warm and nourished 💛
