The Infinity Symbol (∞)

The infinity symbol (∞), also known as the lemniscate, represents the concept of something that is limitless, endless, and without bound.

Meaning and History:

  • Mathematical Origin: Introduced by John Wallis in 1655, it's used to describe quantities larger than any natural number.
  • Symbolic Meaning: It metaphorically represents eternity, everlasting love, and empowerment due to its continuous loop shape.
  • Ancient Roots: The concept is seen in ancient symbols like the Ouroboros (a serpent eating its own tail), representing eternal return and immortality.
  • Modern Usage: It's popular in jewelry and tattoos to symbolize an unending bond and is used in modern mysticism.
