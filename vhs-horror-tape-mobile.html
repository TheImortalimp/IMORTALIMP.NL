A tribute to ADAM TRISTAR — and to every VHS HORROR TAPE(S) that ever hissed in the dark. Following “The Red Pill” and “Suicide Neighborhood”, VHS Horror Tape marks the next chapter in the sonic universe of Dutch synthwave wizard Adam Tristar (Daniel Spiekerman, Amsterdam): darker tones, gritty analog textures and a strong cinematic horror influence — late-night VHS aesthetics, neon-lit nightmares and retro-futuristic suspense. Released 23 January 2026 via Oraculo Records — 300 copies on 180 gr. vinyl, remastered for vinyl by Daniel Hallhuber at Young & Cold Studios.
